## The short version

CleanBG has no upload endpoint. When you remove a background, the AI model runs inside your browser on your own device. Your image, the mask and the result never leave your computer or phone. We cannot see, store or recover your images because they are never transmitted to us.

## What happens when you use the tool

1. **Model download.** The first time you use CleanBG on a device, your browser downloads the neural network weights (about 100–220 MB) from our content delivery network. This is an ordinary static file download; the request contains no information about your images.
2. **Local processing.** Your image is decoded in a Web Worker in your browser, the model computes an alpha mask, and the transparent PNG is composed on a canvas — all in memory on your device.
3. **Caching.** The browser stores the model in its Cache Storage so later visits work offline. You can clear it at any time from your browser settings.

You can verify this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, watch the Network tab while processing an image, or disconnect from the internet after the model has loaded.

## Data we do collect

- **Server logs.** Like every website, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) records standard request logs — IP address, user agent, requested page and time — for security and abuse prevention. These logs never contain image data.
- **Analytics.** Google Analytics 4 counts page views on cleanbg.app. Google receives the page URL, a client identifier, approximate location and browser type, and may set a cookie. We do not send filenames, image dimensions or pixels.
- **Anonymous engine diagnostics.** If a model fails to start, the browser may POST a tiny JSON payload to `/e` with the engine name (for example `ben2:webgpu`), a short error class, and whether the failure was persistent. This contains no filenames, image dimensions or pixels. We use it only to see which browsers cannot run a given model.
- **Local storage.** CleanBG stores a small amount of data in your browser (`localStorage` and Cache Storage) to remember your language, theme and which model configuration works on your device. This stays on your device.

## Data we do not collect

We do not require accounts, do not ask for your name or email, and do not store your images or results. We do not sell personal data. Page-view measurements are sent to Google Analytics as described above.

## Cookies

CleanBG sets one functional cookie to remember your language preference. Google Analytics may set its own cookie to count visits. No advertising cookies are set. If ads are introduced in the future, this policy and the cookie notice will be updated first.

## Children

CleanBG is a general-purpose utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the latest revision.

<h2 id="models">Model credits</h2>

CleanBG is built on open models released under the MIT license. The weights are downloaded to your browser and remain under their original licenses.

- **BEN2** (Background Erase Network 2) by PramaLLC — [github.com/PramaLLC/BEN2](https://github.com/PramaLLC/BEN2). We serve a converted copy of the [onnx-community/BEN2-ONNX](https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/BEN2-ONNX) export with fp32 compute for WebGPU compatibility.
- **BiRefNet** by Zheng Peng et al. — [github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet](https://github.com/ZhengPeng7/BiRefNet), browser-ready 512px export [studioludens/birefnet-lite-512](https://huggingface.co/studioludens/birefnet-lite-512).
- **Transformers.js** and **ONNX Runtime Web** run the models in the browser (Apache-2.0 / MIT).

<h2 id="heic">HEIC photos</h2>

Safari can decode iPhone HEIC files natively. Other browsers load a local copy of [heic-to](https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to) / libheif (LGPL-3.0) **only when you open a HEIC file**. Conversion happens in your tab; the photo is not sent to us. Full notice and source links: [Licenses](/licenses).
